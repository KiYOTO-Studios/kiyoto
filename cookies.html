Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Zero Tracking Guarantee: KiYOTO does not use advertising cookies, profiling cookies, or invasive analytics trackers on this website. Because we do not store tracking cookies on your device, intrusive cookie consent banners are not required.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are commonly used to remember preferences, facilitate logins, or track online behavior across websites.

2. How KiYOTO Uses Cookies & Storage

We believe in building clean, respectful web experiences. On this coming-soon website:

  • No Marketing Cookies: We do not deploy Facebook Pixels, Google Ads trackers, retargeting beacons, or behavioral fingerprinting scripts.
  • No Third-Party Analytics Cookies: We do not use third-party analytics cookies to track your activity across the web.
  • Local Functional Storage: We may use browser localStorage solely to remember that you have already submitted your email on this device, preventing redundant prompts and ensuring a smooth interface.

3. Do We Need a Cookie Banner?

Under the EU ePrivacy Directive and GDPR guidelines, websites that only use strictly necessary technical storage and do not employ tracking or non-essential cookies are legally exempt from displaying obstructive cookie consent pop-ups. We honor your digital space by keeping the site free of intrusive pop-ups.

4. How to Manage Cookies in Your Browser

You can manage or clear cookies and local storage directly through your browser settings at any time (e.g., in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge settings under Privacy and Security).
